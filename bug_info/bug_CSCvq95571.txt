---Start of Note Titled: Eng-note (Created 08/20/2019 23:02:23 Added 08/20/2019 23:02:23 by vasagraw)---
When getaddrinfo() does not fetch DNS resolution in 3 seconds via blocking call, there is a mechanism to spawn an ASYNC job for it. When the ASYNC job concludes, the resolution is kept in cache for later use and the cache is kept refreshed periodically.

In the problem case reported by customer when getaddrinfo() fails in a blocking call, we are unable to spawn the ASYNC call as per the design.

Tweaked the problem code to verify the RCA and RADIUS AUTH started working with it. Given the complex design of currently available DNS Cache, we would investigate a little more before finalizing the fix.. just to ensure we do not break any other working code.
---End of Note Titled: Eng-note---
