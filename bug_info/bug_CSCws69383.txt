On 20 Jan 2026, at 19:37, Vasu Agrawal (vasagraw) <vasagraw@cisco.com> wrote:

Hi Adam,

In the sample config below: 
1) If two authentications fail within 60 seconds, ?permit1? ACL gets applied on MGMT and VTY for a quiet-period of 120 seconds; the login from CONSOLE and SSH/TELNET/NXAPI from IP 173.39.51.143 should still be allowed.
2) Due to CSCws69383, login from CONSOLE and from IP 173.39.51.143 would NOT be possible either.
3) The new instances of failing authentication (by a cracker or a buggy script), during quiet-period , would renew the quiet-period to 120 seconds each time. In this way, new LOGIN would not be possible at all. If no other active session is available, there would be no recovery.

LOGIN-BLOCK config, which was supposed to prevent from DoS attack on authentication service, would only make the attack more effective due to the presence of CSCws69383 - Even CONSOLE login becomes unavailable.
