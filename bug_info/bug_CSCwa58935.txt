0x31463479:     Cannot access memory at address 0x31463479
(gdb) 

Now the questions is who assigned "ai"? We can see it is an argument, for "freeaddrinfo"

(gdb) info args
ai = 0x31463479
(gdb)

(gdb) f 1
#1  0x1012ae56 in get_destination_socket (server=0x1107fa5c, accounting=false, radius_request_state=0x1107d388, curr_ai_family=0xffeca6c8) at ../feature/security/radius/radius_client.c:282

(gdb) p res
$5 = (struct addrinfo *) 0x31463479
